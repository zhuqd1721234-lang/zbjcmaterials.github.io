ABOUT ZBJC MATERIALS

Engineering the Future with Quality Concrete

Established in September 2006 with a registered capital of 11 million RMB, Zibo Zhongbo Building Materials Co., Ltd. (ZBJC Materials) has evolved through 18 years of dedication into a premier industrial leader. Today, we hold fixed assets exceeding 30 million RMB and operate from a massive 45,000 m² state-of-the-art manufacturing facility.

Our annual production capacity for landscape and infrastructure bricks has reached 800,000 m². We have achieved full-scale Standardization, Systematization, and Large-scale Production. Our product quality and aesthetic standards remain at the forefront of the domestic and international industry.
